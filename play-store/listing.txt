FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
Need to make a quick decision? Settle it with a coin flip.

Coin Flip gives you a fair, 50/50 coin toss with a smooth 3D flip animation and a satisfying buzz when it lands. Tap the coin or the Flip button and watch it spin.

FEATURES
• Fair results: every flip uses a secure random number generator
• Realistic 3D flip-and-toss animation with haptic feedback
• Live stats: heads and tails counts with percentages
• Current streak and longest streak tracking
• History of your last 30 flips
• Light and dark mode, with colors that match your phone (Android 12+)
• Reset your stats anytime

PICK YOUR COIN
Choose gold, silver, bronze or rose gold, and change the sides to Yes/No or your own words.

No account needed. Your stats stay on your phone. Optional one-time purchase removes ads.

Great for settling arguments, picking who goes first, making decisions, or just testing your luck.
