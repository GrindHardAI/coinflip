Privacy Policy for Coin Flip

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Coin Flip is a simple coin-flipping app. This policy explains what information is used and why.

Information the app itself stores

  • Your flip statistics and settings (coin style, side labels, sound and shake preferences) are stored only on your device. If you have Android backup turned on, they may be included in your device's own backup, which Google manages under its own policies. You can erase your statistics at any time with the in-app Reset button, or erase everything by uninstalling the app.
  • We, the developer, do not run any servers and do not receive your personal information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • The free version of the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

  • If you buy "Remove ads", the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether you own the purchase.

Children

  • Coin Flip is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Permissions

  • Vibration, for haptic feedback; internet and network state, to load ads and verify purchases.

Changes

  • If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
