# Privacy Policy for Coin Flip

_Last updated: November 16, 2026_

Coin Flip does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

- The app has no internet access and doesn't contain ads, analytics or tracking.
- Your flip statistics are stored only on your device. If you have Android backup
  turned on, they may be included in your device's own backup, which Google manages
  under its own policies. You can erase them at any time with the in-app **Reset**
  button or by uninstalling the app.
- The only permission the app uses is vibration, for haptic feedback.
